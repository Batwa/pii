How it works

Four steps, start to clean file.

No configuration required — the defaults are safe. Adjust only if you want to.

  1. Choose your file type

    Pick CSV/tabular data, text documents or images. Each track is tuned for that format.

  2. Upload your file

    Drag and drop, or browse. Multiple files are supported for text and images.

  3. We scan and redact

    Presidio, spaCy, custom regex and OCR find the PII; your chosen strategy hides it.

  4. Download clean results

    Take the redacted file plus a plain-language privacy report of everything that changed.

About

About Privacy Sandbox

A local-first privacy tool for anyone who has to work with real data.

🎯 Project mission

Privacy Sandbox helps researchers, students, developers and business users work safely with real-world datasets that contain sensitive personal information. The tool automatically detects and redacts PII so teams can collaborate, publish and train models while staying compliant with privacy regulations.

🛠️ Technology behind it

Detection

  • Microsoft Presidio
  • spaCy NER
  • custom regex patterns
  • Tesseract OCR

Faces are found by a dual-model MediaPipe detector with an automatic Haar-cascade fallback if the primary pass returns nothing.

Processing

  • pandas + NumPy
  • OpenCV
  • Pillow

Supported formats: CSV, TXT, JSON, MD, JPG, JPEG, PNG, BMP.

📜 Privacy & compliance

An automated, documented redaction step helps you meet the requirements of:

  • GDPR — General Data Protection Regulation
  • FERPA — Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act
  • HIPAA — Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act
  • CCPA — California Consumer Privacy Act

🔒 Data security guarantees

Local processing

Files are processed on your machine. No data is sent to an external server.

No permanent storage

Working files exist only for the duration of the scan and are deleted afterwards.

Multiple strategies

Choose the level of redaction that fits your risk profile, per file type.

Audit trail

Every scan can produce a detailed report of what was found and what was changed.
